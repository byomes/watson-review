### Ch.4 — What AI Actually Is *(new chapter)*
Plain-language explainer chapter. Locked explanation chain (finalized 2026-09-17): opens with **token prediction as the frame ("guessing, not talking")** — AI doesn't think or speak, it probabilistically predicts the next most-likely chunk of language — then unpacks the mechanism as a five-step chain: (1) human enters a prompt, (2) the language model reads/processes it, (3) intent detection, (4) skill evaluation (does the model have a tool matching the detected intent — added as its own distinct step 2026-09-17), (5) tool execution. Includes a worked Genesis 1:1 tokenization example for lay readers (breaking "beginning" into "begin"/"ning," "created" into "creat"/"ed," to show tokens are chunks, not words-as-understood). Personal material: the "ChatGPT search felt like Google on steroids" first-contact moment; the "flux capacitor" moment (Back to the Future reference) — realizing AI processing has discrete steps (understand → respond → sometimes use a tool), which is what triggered Bill's decision to build his own assistant, Watson, from scratch rather than use a frontier model, specifically to control theological accuracy; the Watson-build hallucination story — Bill deliberately red-teamed candidate models by claiming to have preached a sermon he never preached, one model invented a sermon on the spot, which showed the no-hallucination guardrail wasn't strong enough yet; the resulting hard-stop rule, confirmed firing in real use: "I cannot find an answer to that question, please contact Doctor Bill for further inquiries"; the nickname/alias story (had to manually teach Watson "Bill" = William, "Jen" = Jennifer, since a from-scratch model doesn't have internet-scale training to infer that for free). The Genesis-to-Joshua ~470-year AI chronology calculation (from Bill's own Joshua 7 sermon, where he hedged the AI's number live and unprompted) belongs here as a minor, non-load-bearing research-check example — distinct from the two-AI-engines story, which was deliberately placed in Ch.7 instead (see below). Three citation candidates pulled for the explanation chain (DevelopersIO and Medium on token prediction, Decagon on intent detection) are logged but **unverified** — Bill's standing rule requires he personally confirm every field-engagement quote/citation before it's used.

### Ch.11 — The Question You Carry Out the Door
Closing chapter. Frames the four guardrails as a real-time gut-check scene — the actual moment of picking up the phone to open an AI tool — with a warning sign for each:
